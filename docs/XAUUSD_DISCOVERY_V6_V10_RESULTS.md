# XAUUSD Discovery V6–V10 — 2026-10-24

**Research only; no trading promotion.** These are independently simulated experiments on the user's historical XAUUSD Bid/Ask archive (17 annual JSON files, 2010–2026-09-20, 397,299 aggregated M15 bars). The full Python scripts, 47 archive artifacts, CSV trial ledgers and eleven passing unit tests were delivered as a research ZIP in the project conversation. No live broker orders were placed.

Methodology: Discovery 2010–2017, confirmation 2018–2021, and 2022–September 2026 historical diagnostics. **The final era has been viewed before and is not blind OOS.** Baseline simulation uses next M15 bid/ask open, $0.10/oz slippage per side, $0.05/oz roundtrip fee, spread cap $5/oz, adverse stop-first resolution; missing broker financing and lot economics remain blockers.

- **V6, eight distinct families and portfolio rotation:** equal-8 family portfolio: −7.80R / 16 of 48 positive confirmation months; +0.13R / 23 of 57 positive diagnostic months. Fixed dev-positive family basket: −7.64R confirmation; +5.44R / 26 of 57 diagnostic. Causal rolling top-3 on last 24 complete shadow months: −8.12R confirmation; −2.39R / 28 of 57 diagnostic months. A higher win-month count did NOT translate to net profit. Portfolio is a risk-budgeted independent-strategy overlay, not broker netting execution.
- **V7, probabilistic model:** 20 mathematical price/volatility/spread/time features; 4-hour forecast, logistic model fit on 2010–14, least-bad entry threshold selected on 2015–17, refit through 2017 for confirmation, through 2021 for recent diagnostics. AUC 0.5056 (confirm) and 0.5087 (diagnostic), no predictive skill shown. Confirm −2.51R / 14 of 48; diagnostic +0.56R / 6 of 57; higher costs −2.52R. Reject.
- **V8, 240 weekday/UTC-hour schedules:** five met discovery eligibility but zero passed confirmation. The best dev-selected Friday afternoon long rule achieved +49.04R development but −23.45R confirmation and −14.26R diagnostic. Reject as overfit.
- **V9, quality and feasibility:** median M15 entry spread $0.34/oz, p99 $1.22/oz; 140 bars with spreads exceeding $5. 6,091 Sunday bars and 88 on 2010-01-01 require broker/feed verification. Monthly long-only baseline was positive 34/57 recent months (no financing). The causal top-three overlay had simultaneous opposing directions during 1.79% of its active trading time. No broker netting proof.
- **V10, environment sensitivity:** Locked V4 z-score candidate gave 23–24/57 positive diagnostic months and +3.76 to +6.06R across spread-cap/gap scenarios. Removing Sunday quote bars and recomputing features gave +10.42R and 28/57 positive recent months, but this **must not be selected** based on inspected diagnostics. Brokerage calendar cannot yet be authenticated.
- **Regression testing:** 11/11 tests passed for bar-completion causality, gap handling, next-bar bid/ask fills, adverse intrabar execution, month-end ledger attribution, and causal feature invariance.

**Conclusion:** None of the tested methods demonstrates positive returns every month; several initially promising variants fail later eras or after realistic cost stress. Keep all failures, require verified symbol contract size, financing and broker trading-day rollover, add XAUUSD to FX-only engine schema, create a single-account risk/netting simulator, and pre-register candidate versions before any truly new months of prospective shadow trading. No guaranteed profits, no live trades.
