## Deliberately not claimed as complete

- No real-money live trading. Real and contest MT5 accounts are structurally blocked.
- No automatic broker execution from a strategy signal. The optional order form is
  manual, demo-only, disabled by default, and separately audited.
- No guarantee of profit or market prediction.
- No strategy can be called statistically valid until it passes the required gates on
  adequate real historical data and later completes time-based shadow and paper trials.
- Browser continuous STT/TTS works while the page is open after one required permission
  click. A system-wide listener that runs while the browser is closed is not installed.
- News, sentiment, macro calendar, funding/open-interest, options, tick depth, true
  order-book, and trade-flow models require licensed/time-valid data sources. They are
  registered capabilities and blueprints, not fabricated inputs.
- The dependency-light Gaussian HMM is calibrated independently on each pair/timeframe
  with Baum-Welch EM, and the GMM volatility filter is implemented. Markov-switching GARCH,
  multi-asset Johansen/PCA/HRP, exchange-level microstructure, and RL remain gated until
  suitable aligned datasets exist.
- Atlas still uses deterministic scheduling rather than LLM-based portfolio prioritization;
  this is intentionally deferred until a measurable, auditable objective is specified.

The code is complete for the safe demo-research boundary. Profitability and statistical
validity are not code-completion claims: they require adequate broker history and later
time-based shadow/paper evidence. Expansion must remain versioned, tested, and subject
to the constitution.
